Metaball

Five colour layers of soft blobs, each blurred and alpha-thresholded back into one hard edge. Where two halos touch they fuse; where the layers overlap, the blend mode makes a colour neither of them had.

Fusion
30

Blur radius. Sets how far apart two blobs still fuse — roughly twice this in pixels. Scales with blob size automatically.

20

Alpha gain in the colour matrix. Low is a soft cloud, high is a crisp liquid edge that starts to alias past ~36.

−9

Alpha bias. Toward −16 the blobs shrink and merge later, reading tight and separate; toward −3 they swell and fuse early.

0.25

A final sub-pixel blur after the threshold. Takes the staircase off the edge without softening the shape.

The field is drawn at this square size and scaled up to fill the viewport, so a full-screen blur never repaints full-screen. Drop it if the frame rate sags.

Filter engine

SVG thresholds alpha only, so layer colour survives intact. CSS blur() contrast() is the Safari fallback — it hits colour too, so layers get an opaque ground and only Screen composites cleanly.
